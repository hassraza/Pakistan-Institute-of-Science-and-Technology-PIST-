info Document Upload Guidelines & Requirements

  • Supported Formats: PDF (.pdf), JPEG (.jpg, .jpeg), PNG (.png), and WebP (.webp). Maximum file size: 5MB per document.
  • Legibility: Ensure candidate name, father's name, roll number, and obtained marks are sharp and unobstructed.
  • Verification Standards: The admissions board verifies transcripts against original board registers before issuing merit list positions.
  • Prohibited: Do not submit cropped edges, blurred smartphone scans, or password-protected PDF files.
