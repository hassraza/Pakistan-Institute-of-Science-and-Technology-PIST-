Notice Regarding Fee Challan Submission Deadline for Candidates on 1st Merit List (Fall 2025)

Issued Date July 15, 2025
Authority Office of Registrar
Target Audience 1st Merit Selected (1,240)
Dispatch Ratio 1,234 (99.5%) / 6 Fail
Delivery Progress (Email & SMS Gateway sync) 99.5% confirmed receipt

It is notified for the information of all candidates who have been provisionally offered admission to undergraduate degree programs at the Pakistan Institute of Science and Technology (PIST) in the Fall 2025 Semester under the 1st Merit List that the deadline for completing admission formalities has been established.

1. Deposit of Semester Dues:

Eligible applicants must generate their customized Fee Challan from the PIST Admissions Portal and deposit all required dues at any designated nationwide online branch of Bank Alfalah Limited on or before July 24, 2025, no later than 04:00 PM PST.

2. Proof of Payment Upload:

Immediately subsequent to payment, candidates are strictly instructed to scan the "PIST University Copy" bearing the bank branch stamp, transaction sequence code, and deposit date, and upload the high-resolution copy to their individual candidate dashboard.

3. Forfeiture of Seat Allocation:

Failure to deposit dues or submit verified payment receipts by the prescribed date and time shall result in automatic cancellation of the provisional admission offer without further individual notice. Forfeited seats shall forthwith be made available to waiting candidates in the 2nd Merit List scheduled for publication on July 27, 2025.

By order of the Academic Council & Central Admissions Committee, PIST Main Secretariat, Sector H-12, Islamabad.
