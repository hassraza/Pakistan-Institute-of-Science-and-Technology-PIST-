Financial Aid & Scholarships

PIST is committed to ensuring that financial barriers do not prevent exceptional students from pursuing their academic goals. We offer a comprehensive range of merit-based, need-based, and external scholarships.

Scholarship Categories

workspace_premium

Merit-Based

Awarded to top academic performers. Covers up to 100% of tuition fees.

  • GPA ≥ 3.8 / 4.0
  • Top 5% of admission cohort
  • Renewable annually based on performance
volunteer_activism

Need-Based

Support for students demonstrating significant financial need. Requires detailed documentation.

  • Income assessment required
  • Covers tuition and stipends
  • Annual review process
school

HEC Scholarships

Funded by the Higher Education Commission for targeted disciplines and demographics.

  • Subject to HEC criteria
  • Specific to certain degree programs
  • External application required
public

International

Exclusive funding opportunities for international students joining PIST.

  • Non-Pakistani nationality
  • Strong academic background
  • English proficiency required

Application Process

1

Admission Setup

Complete your primary admission application to generate your PIST ID.

2

Portal Access

Log into the Financial Aid Portal using your new credentials.

3

Documentation

Upload required transcripts, tax returns, and recommendation letters.

4

Review & Award

Committee reviews applications. Awards are announced alongside admission decisions.
