Program Overview

The BS Computer Science program at PIST offers a comprehensive curriculum that balances theoretical foundations with practical application. Students engage deeply with algorithms, data structures, artificial intelligence, and software systems engineering. Our pedagogical approach emphasizes rigorous problem-solving and critical thinking, preparing graduates to tackle complex computational challenges across diverse industries.

The department fosters a culture of innovation, encouraging students to participate in undergraduate research, hackathons, and collaborative projects with faculty members who are leaders in their respective fields.

Learning Outcomes

  • check_circle

    Computational Foundation

    Apply knowledge of computing and mathematics appropriate to the discipline.

  • check_circle

    Problem Analysis

    Analyze a problem, and identify and define the computing requirements appropriate to its solution.

  • check_circle

    System Design

    Design, implement, and evaluate a computer-based system, process, component, or program to meet desired needs.
