Eligibility Criteria

  • Minimum 60% marks in HSSC (Pre-Engineering/Pre-Medical/Computer Science) or equivalent.
  • A-Level students must provide an Equivalence Certificate from IBCC.
  • Valid score in the PIST Entry Test or an approved standardized test (NAT/HAT).
  • For specific engineering programs, adherence to PEC guidelines is mandatory.

Application Process

1

Online Registration

Create an account on the PIST Admissions Portal and fill out your basic profile information.

2

Document Upload

Scan and upload all required educational documents, CNIC/B-Form, and a recent photograph.

3

Application Fee Submission

Generate the fee challan and deposit the non-refundable processing fee at any designated bank branch.

4

Entry Test

Download your admit card and appear for the PIST Entry Test on the scheduled date.
